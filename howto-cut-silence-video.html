How to automatically cut silence and pauses from a video on iPhone

Tightcut finds the gaps where nobody speaks and lists them. You check what will go, then export once.

What happens to your clip

Tightcut reads the sound of the video and treats the parts that stay below a loudness level as gaps where nobody speaks. Before anything is cut, it shows those parts as a list, and you can bring any of them back one row at a time.

Steps in Tightcut

  1. First screenTap Choose from Photos for a video in your photo library, or Choose from Files for one in the Files app.
  2. Steps screenWhen the file is read, Remove silence is turned on and the dark green card at the top shows Original, Result and Shorter by.
  3. Remove silence › settingsTap the text of Remove silence. Parts to remove lists every part that will be cut, with its start and end time. Tap a row to keep that part.
  4. DetectionIf too much or too little is picked up, set Sensitivity to Strict, Standard or Loose. To set it precisely, turn on Set the level myself. If the start or end of words gets clipped, widen Breathing room, then tap Done.
  5. ExportTap Export at the bottom. When Export finished appears, choose Save to Photos, Save to Files or Share.

Under Also in the same export on the same screen you can also turn on Change the background and Play backwards. Everything you pick runs in one export.

Questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Reading the file, finding the silence and exporting all happen on your iPhone. Tightcut never connects to the network.

Can I see what will be cut before exporting?

Yes. Open the settings of Remove silence: Parts to remove lists each part with its start, end and length. Tap a row to keep that part.

Will it clip the start or end of my words?

Breathing room keeps a little sound before and after each cut. If words still get clipped, widen it.

Does exporting lower the quality?

The resolution and frame rate of your file are kept.
