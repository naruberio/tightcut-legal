How to trim silent pauses from a podcast or recording on iPhone

Take out only the gaps where nobody speaks. You decide what goes from a list, before anything is exported.

Same flow for audio-only files

Podcast sessions, interviews, lecture or meeting recordings: material without a picture goes into Tightcut too. It picks up the parts that stay below a loudness level and lists them under Parts to remove.

Steps in Tightcut

  1. First screenTap Choose from Files and pick an audio file in the Files app.
  2. Steps screenRemove silence is turned on and the card at the top shows Original, Result and Shorter by.
  3. Remove silence › settingsTap the text of Remove silence and check the parts under Parts to remove. Tap a row to keep that part.
  4. DetectionUse Sensitivity (Strict, Standard, Loose) and Breathing room to decide how tight it gets, then tap Done.
  5. ExportTap Export. When Export finished appears, tap Share and choose where to send or save the file.

Questions

Does it work with audio files, not just videos?

Yes. With Choose from Files you can pick an audio file in the Files app.

Is my recording uploaded anywhere?

No. Finding the silence and exporting both happen on your iPhone. Tightcut never connects to the network.

Will every thinking pause be removed?

Before you export, the parts to be removed are listed under Parts to remove. Tap a row to keep a pause you want.

Will words get cut off at the joins?

Breathing room keeps a little sound before and after each cut.
