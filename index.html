Tightcut

Tightcut is an iPhone app that drops the parts of a clip where nobody speaks, so a long take becomes a short one. It can also change the background behind a person and play a clip backwards, as optional steps of the same export. One-time purchase — no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, no watermark. Everything runs on your device; the app never connects to the network.

Tightcut は、しゃべっていない間を落として動画を短くする iPhone アプリです。 背景の差し替えと逆再生も、同じ書き出しの中で任意に行えます。買い切りで、通信は一切行いません。
