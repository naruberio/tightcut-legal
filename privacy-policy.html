Privacy Policy — Tightcut

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Naruberio LLC ("we", "us", "our") provides this Privacy Policy to explain how the iOS application Tightcut (the "App") handles your information.

The short version. Tightcut runs entirely on your iPhone. It has no account, no server of ours, and never connects to the network. Your video is read, analysed, and written back out on the device. Because we receive nothing, there is nothing for us to lose, sell, or hand over.

1. Information we collect

None. The App collects no personal information.

We declare "Data Not Collected" in the App Privacy details on the App Store and in the App's privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy). We do not track you, and the App shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to track.

2. Where your files are stored

On your device. While a job runs, the App writes intermediate files to its own temporary directory and deletes them when the job finishes — and again at the next launch, in case the App was interrupted. The finished file is kept in Application Support until you save or share it; only the most recent one is kept, and it is excluded from device backups.

The App does not use iCloud sync.

3. Permissions

PermissionWhen it is requestedCan you decline?
Photos (add only)Only when you save a finished video to your photo libraryYes. Use Share or Save to Files instead

The App asks for add-only access, which lets it write a file to your library but not read it. When you pick a clip from Photos, the system picker runs outside the App and hands over only the one file you chose, so the App never sees the rest of your library.

The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, Health, or Bluetooth access.

4. How your clip is processed

  • Removing silence. The App measures the loudness of the audio over time, on the device, to decide which stretches count as silence. It does not transcribe speech, recognise words, or identify speakers.
  • Changing the background. Person detection uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. The green-screen option compares colours. No frame leaves the device, and no image is used to train anything.
  • Playing backwards. Frames and audio are re-written locally in reverse order.

Everything you pick runs in a single export on your device. Nothing is queued, uploaded, or processed remotely.

5. Third parties

There are none. The App contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase, and the like), no advertising networks, and no crash-reporting services. It makes no network requests of any kind.

Apple processes your purchase of the App through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to that transaction: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

6. Purchases

Tightcut is a paid app with a single up-front price. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no advertising.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / APPI)

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or delete on request.

Everything the App stores is under your control on your device. Deleting the App removes all of its data from your device.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may revise this Policy when the law or the App changes. The "Last updated" date above changes accordingly, and significant changes will be noted on this page.
