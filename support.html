Frequently asked questions

It did not cut anything.

Your background noise is probably louder than the level Tightcut treats as silence. Set Sensitivity to Loose, which accepts a louder room as quiet. If that is still not enough, turn on Set the level myself in the Detection section and raise the level until the quiet parts appear in the list.

It said everything was silence.

The opposite case: the whole clip sits below the level. Set Sensitivity to Strict, or lower the level by hand.

It cut in the middle of a word.

Increase Breathing room. It keeps a little sound before and after each cut, so words are not clipped.

I want to keep one of the cuts.

In Parts to remove, tap the row you want to keep. It stays in the finished clip. The Shorter by figure updates as you change your mind.

Why can I not reverse a clip with a transparent background?

Reversing rewrites every frame, and the transparency does not survive that rewrite — the result would quietly come out opaque. Rather than hand you a broken file, Tightcut refuses the combination and says so. Pick a colour, a photo, or a blur as the background if you also want to play the clip backwards.

The exported file looks re-encoded.

Some source formats cannot be written out without re-encoding, and every step other than removing silence has to redraw the picture. Tightcut re-encodes rather than failing, and keeps the size and frame rate of your original.

Saving to Photos failed.

Tightcut asks for add-only access to your photo library. If you declined it, open the iOS Settings app, find Tightcut, and allow adding photos — or use Share or Save to Files instead, which need no permission at all.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Use Apple's refund request page.
