2. What the App is — and what it is not

Tightcut is an editing tool. Its main job is to find the stretches where nobody speaks and remove the ones you approve. It can also replace the background behind a person and play a clip backwards, as optional steps of the same export.

Detection is an estimate. What counts as silence depends on background noise, the microphone, and the sensitivity and level you set. Review the detected cuts before exporting — you can keep any of them. Background detection is likewise an estimate and depends on lighting, contrast, and what is in frame.

The App does not transcribe speech, generate subtitles, or edit your clip for you beyond the steps you pick.
