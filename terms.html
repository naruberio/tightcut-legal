The job Tightcut is built for

Tightcut shortens recordings of people talking. It measures how loud the sound is from moment to moment, lists the gaps where nobody is talking, and on export removes each gap you have not marked to keep. Two extra steps can ride along in that same export if you switch them on: swapping what is behind a person, and running the clip from its end to its start.

It does not turn speech into text, make captions, or change your clip in any way you did not pick.

You need an iPhone with iOS 17.0 or newer. On other hardware or older software we cannot promise it will work.

A cut list is a first draft

Which stretches count as quiet is Tightcut's estimate. Room noise, the microphone you recorded with, and the sensitivity or level you choose all shift the line between “quiet” and “talking”. Look over the list before you export: any gap on it can be kept instead. Telling a person apart from the background is an estimate as well, and depends on the light, the contrast, and whatever else is in the shot.
